Solar System

Animated view of our cosmic neighborhood

Planets

  • Mercury: Closest to the Sun, smallest planet
  • Venus: Hottest planet, similar size to Earth
  • Earth: Our home, only known planet with life
  • Mars: The Red Planet, target for exploration
  • Jupiter: Largest planet, gas giant
  • Saturn: Famous for its rings
  • Uranus: Ice giant, rotates on its side
  • Neptune: Farthest from the Sun, strong winds
